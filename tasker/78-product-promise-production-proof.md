# 78 — Product promise production-proof program

**Created:** 2026-08-31

**Status:** Research-first launch gate — synthesize existing evidence, close proof gaps through the
owning trackers, and produce a paid-launch decision

**Priority:** P0 for paid launch

**Type:** Product reliability research, end-to-end verification, and launch acceptance

## Initial WP-0/WP-1 baseline — 2026-08-31

The first claim inventory, bounded cohort proposal, reusable longitudinal fixture, ten-journey
matrix, owned blocker ledger, and baseline decision are now recorded in
[the production-proof baseline](../docs/product/product-promise-production-proof-baseline-2026-08-31.md).

**Current decision: NO-GO for paid launch.** This is an evidence reconciliation, not a claim that
the product lacks implementation. The immediate independent blockers are:

1. Tasker 76's confirmed anonymous access to privileged Production database functions;
2. Tasker 77's unratified and internally inconsistent commercial contract;
3. Tasker 70's open isolated repetitions and full nine-scenario zero-retry battery, plus Tasker
   20's Production lifecycle flag remaining off pending the Preview detach/reconcile smoke; and
4. no retained fresh-account longitudinal receipt spanning registration through later re-entry,
   billing/account control, deletion, and two-user isolation.

The initial pass also routes two known Production calendar defects to Tasker 74, generated-content
and responsive failures to Tasker 79, Today/Brief work to Taskers 27/52, and fresh-account live
branches to Tasker 38. No P0/P1 gap identified in the baseline is intentionally unowned.

### Proposed decisions awaiting founder ratification

- **Promise:** “An individual creator can give BuildOS the messy version of one real project,
  inspect and correct the structured project it creates, return later to a useful next move, and
  let a permitted agent read or change that same project only with grounded context and a truthful
  receipt.”
- **First paid cohort:** invited, English-speaking adult individual author-operators or working indie
  authors with one active book/book-adjacent project that has real value at risk; single-owner,
  private, non-regulated workflows.
- **Support matrix:** latest desktop Chrome/Edge plus real iOS Safari and Android Chrome checks at
  the 390/768/1440 visual breakpoints. The current guide only names desktop Chrome/Edge as the best
  experience, so mobile support is proposed and unproven.

### First claim dispositions

- Keep text/paste capture only after the fresh-account journey passes; treat voice as preview until
  its mobile/desktop transcript, retention, and cleanup path is proven.
- Do not sell external-agent/shared-state or general Agentic Chat writes as guaranteed until the
  Tasker 70/20 release gates close.
- Narrow general calendar/task-sync claims to the exact verified direct event route or close Tasker
  74 first.
- Remove or qualify “Unlimited projects,” broad “Data export,” and “Priority email support” until
  Tasker 77 defines the actual limits, export surface, and monitored support promise.
- Preserve only the current no-charge statement while billing is disabled; trial, price, grace,
  refund, and entitlement copy awaits Tasker 77 ratification.

## Kernel

BuildOS has strong automated coverage and substantial production functionality, but there is no
single artifact proving that the promise presented to a new customer works coherently from first
visit through repeated use. Agentic Chat has a detailed production battery, yet its final release
gate remains open. Fresh-account onboarding, brain-dump capture, calendar mutations, daily briefs,
re-entry, failure recovery, billing, and account deletion have evidence in different places or
still have live-verification debt.

This tasker does not duplicate implementation owned by existing trackers. It defines the product
promise, turns it into a bounded set of golden customer journeys, locates the best existing proof,
and routes every failed gate to the narrowest owning tracker. Its final output is the evidence-backed
decision that BuildOS is safe to sell—not merely that its test suite is green.

## Working product promise to ratify

> A person can unload a messy thought, receive useful organized work, return later without losing
> context, and trust BuildOS or its agent to read and change the right things without inventing,
> silently dropping, or exposing their data.

Research the homepage, pricing, onboarding, documentation, demos, lifecycle messages, and founder
intent. Ratify or replace the sentence before using it as the launch contract. Every public claim
must map to a demonstrated journey or be narrowed before launch.

## Golden journey matrix

At minimum, the proof program must cover:

1. **Discover and start:** public promise, registration, authentication, verification, and first
   successful session.
2. **Capture messy input:** submit a representative brain dump and receive the expected project,
   tasks, context, and confirmation without duplication or loss.
3. **Understand the result:** inspect and edit the generated project, tasks, documents, goals,
   phases, and next step with internal references rendered for humans.
4. **Act today:** use Today and Daily Briefs to identify the right next work; refreshes and generated
   summaries remain coherent and recover from partial/stale data.
5. **Ask and delegate:** Agentic Chat reads grounded context, performs commissioned writes, asks
   durable clarification only when necessary, and never claims an unexecuted change.
6. **Schedule work:** connect a calendar, create/update/remove scheduled work and recurrence, and
   reconcile provider and BuildOS state without orphans.
7. **Return later:** sign out/in or begin a later session and find the expected project context,
   conversation state, decisions, and next step intact.
8. **Recover safely:** retry interrupted work, handle provider/timeouts/duplicates, inspect a clear
   failure state, and resume without double mutation or hidden cost.
9. **Pay and control the account:** cross the chosen commercial boundary, activate, manage payment,
   cancel/refund as allowed, export/read data, and delete the account under the ratified contract.
10. **Isolation and privacy:** two-user and anonymous probes cannot read or mutate another user's
    data through UI, API, RPC, realtime, public-sharing, or agent paths.

Each journey must name persona/account state, fixture, exact steps, expected durable state,
telemetry, cleanup, owner, automated coverage, live evidence, last verification date, and result.

## Work packages

### WP-0 — Promise and claim inventory

- Inventory the explicit and implied promises across homepage, pricing, onboarding, docs, demo
  content, emails, and support copy.
- Group them into capture, organization, daily guidance, agent action, calendar, persistence,
  privacy, billing, and account-control promises.
- Ratify the working product-promise sentence and identify claims that should be removed or narrowed.
- Define the target launch cohort and supported browsers/devices so “production-proof” has a bounded
  meaning.

### WP-1 — Evidence reconciliation

- Build the golden-journey matrix and attach the strongest current automated and live receipt to
  every step.
- Consume rather than duplicate work from:
    - [Tasker 20](20-agentic-chat-wave3-security-brief.md) for Agentic Chat security/lifecycle;
    - [Tasker 38](38-live-verification-debt.md) for manual production verification;
    - [Tasker 70](70-agentic-chat-production-battery-remediation.md) for the zero-retry production
      battery;
    - [Tasker 74](74-calendar-legacy-surface-cleanup.md) for calendar contract drift;
    - [Tasker 52](52-ai-inbox-review-loop-remediation.md) for one-brief review behavior;
    - [Tasker 27](27-today-migration-ia-consolidation.md) for Today/IA rollout;
    - [Taskers 76](76-production-database-security-containment.md) and
      [77](77-billing-commercial-contract-reconciliation.md) for security and payment readiness.
- Mark evidence as automated, preview, production, stale, partial, or missing. A source-complete
  implementation is not a production receipt.

### WP-2 — Fresh-account longitudinal run

- Create a clean test user through the same public path as a customer.
- Execute discovery, registration, onboarding, brain dump, project generation, task/document edits,
  Today, Briefs, chat, calendar, re-entry, billing state, export, and deletion in sequence.
- Include a later-session return after context/session caches expire.
- Record timings, telemetry identifiers, durable database state, visible results, and cleanup without
  retaining personal or secret material.
- Repeat at the supported mobile and desktop widths and in every launch browser.

### WP-3 — Core AI production gate

- Finish Tasker 70's isolated repetitions and full nine-scenario, zero-harness-retry battery.
- Finish Tasker 20's detached lifecycle/reconcile verification before enabling its production flag.
- Complete the live prompt-injection, cross-tenant, tool-authorization, clarification, and false
  success tests in Tasker 38.
- Establish acceptable latency, retry, token, cost, and cleanup bounds for each golden AI journey.
- Prove generated content is useful and human-readable, not merely schema-valid.

### WP-4 — Non-AI durability and recovery

- Verify project/task/document/calendar mutations across refresh, logout/login, retry, and concurrent
  tabs.
- Exercise network interruption, provider failure, stale data, expired authentication, duplicate
  submission, and partial-success recovery.
- Confirm every apparent success has a durable receipt and every failure gives a safe next action.
- Verify delete/export/account-control behavior against the commercial and privacy contracts.

### WP-5 — Launch evaluation and operating baseline

- Run the complete matrix on the intended production release with flags and versions recorded.
- Classify every failure as launch-blocking, cohort-limiting, or post-launch debt with an owner.
- Establish dashboards/alerts for registration, capture completion, agent success/failure, calendar
  sync, billing transitions, latency, cost, and support-triggering errors.
- Produce a go/no-go packet with unresolved risk accepted explicitly by the owner.

## Exit condition

1. The founder-ratified product promise and launch cohort are explicit.
2. Every public launch claim maps to a passing golden journey or has been removed/narrowed.
3. A fresh customer account completes the full longitudinal journey on supported mobile and desktop
   environments with durable state and cleanup evidence.
4. The final Agentic Chat zero-retry battery, lifecycle gate, security probes, and cost/latency bounds
   pass.
5. Calendar, Today, Briefs, re-entry, billing/account control, and failure recovery pass their live
   gates.
6. No success message lacks a durable effect, no internal syntax appears as customer copy, and no
   cross-user or anonymous probe succeeds.
7. Monitoring and support can identify, explain, and recover each launch-critical failure class.
8. The final go/no-go packet has no unowned P0/P1 failure and records the owner's launch decision.

## Non-goals

- Reimplementing fixes already owned by another tasker.
- Proving every experimental or admin surface before charging the bounded launch cohort.
- Using one happy-path demo or a green CI run as the production-proof conclusion.
- Requiring a permanently empty backlog; the gate is no unresolved customer-critical risk.
