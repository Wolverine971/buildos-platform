# X — 18 projects, one shared context

**Single post:**

> i have 18 projects open right now.
>
> the problem was never too many ideas. it's losing the context between them every time i switch.
>
> built buildos so each project just remembers where i left off. context switching stopped eating my whole morning.

**(Optional) thread continuation:**

1. the tax nobody counts: the first 20 min of every work block goes to remembering where you left off. what you decided, what's half-done, the next real step.
2. do that a dozen times a day and the day's gone. not to the work — to reloading the work.
3. fix isn't discipline. it's a place that holds the context for you. drop back in, it's already there. that's the whole idea behind buildos.
