A 100-day system for taking a nonfiction book idea from contract to self-published manuscript: backward-planned phases, forward drafting, four revision passes, and self-publishing.

## What this book is
A practical, anti-fragile playbook for men earning under ~$100K/year who want leverage and a way out of the permanent lower class. It blends AI leverage, social dynamics, relationship strategy, and historical pattern-recognition into frameworks the reader can actually apply.

## Core philosophy
- **Plan backward from publication, write forward from the seed.**
- **Anti-fragility is the backbone** — getting stronger under pressure (Taleb), paired with Ferriss-style leverage and practical hacks.
- **Do not polish language you may later delete.**
- **The rough draft's job is not to be good; its job is to make the entire book visible.**

## Production roadmap
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1–7):** Define the Book Contract — target reader, reader promise, one-sentence premise, ending, form & voice, scope cap, exclusions.
2. **Phase 2 (Days 8–15):** Build the Chapter & Framework Blueprint — core frameworks, chapter outline, examples and evidence, continuity/repetition rules.
3. **Phase 3 (Days 16–50):** Drafting engine — 30 writing sessions @ 2,000 words/day plus 5 buffer days = ~60,000 words.
4. **Phase 4 (Days 51–64):** Structural revision — argument flow, causality, chapter sequencing, dead sections.
5. **Phase 5 (Days 65–74):** Reader feedback — 3–5 beta readers.
6. **Phase 6 (Days 75–85):** Language revision — voice, examples, transitions, repetition.
7. **Phase 7 (Days 86–92):** Proof & production — manuscript lock, interior layout, cover, front/back matter.
8. **Phase 8 (Days 93–100):** Upload & release — proofs, defect correction, launch.
