Welcome to your 100-day book workspace. This project implements the backward-planned, forward-drafted fiction production model for a ~60,000-word novel.

## Core Philosophy
- **Plan backward from publication, write forward from the seed.**
- **Do not polish language you may later delete.**
- **The rough draft’s job is not to be good; its job is to make the entire book visible.**

## Production Roadmap
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1–7):** Define the Book Contract (Premise, ending, promise, POV, scope).
2. **Phase 2 (Days 8–15):** Build the Story Blueprint (Characters, beats, 20–30 scene cards, continuity bank).
3. **Phase 3 (Days 16–50):** Drafting Engine (30 writing sessions @ 2,000 wpd + 5 buffer days = 60,000 words).
4. **Phase 4 (Days 51–64):** Structural Revision (Pacing, causality, arcs, dead sections).
5. **Phase 5 (Days 65–74):** Reader Feedback (3–5 beta readers).
6. **Phase 6 (Days 75–85):** Language Revision (Voice, dialogue, transitions, continuity).
7. **Phase 7 (Days 86–92):** Proof & Production (Manuscript lock, interior layout, cover, front/back matter).
8. **Phase 8 (Days 93–100):** Upload & Release (Proofs, defects correction, launch).
